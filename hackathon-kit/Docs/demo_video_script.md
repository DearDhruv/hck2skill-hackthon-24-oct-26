# 2-Minute Demo Video Walkthrough Script

Follow this precise, timed script when recording your 120-second video demo for Hack2Skill judges.

---

## ⏱️ Video Breakdown

### 00:00 - 00:20: The Hook & The Problem
> **Visual**: Show title slide / your face briefly, then cut immediately to the mobile phone screen.  
> **Audio**:  
> *"Hello judges! In fast-moving environments, users cannot afford clunky apps that freeze the second internet access drops or fail when moving from a phone to a desktop. We built **[Project Name]** — an offline-first, multi-device intelligent platform built with Kotlin and Compose Multiplatform that unifies your workflow across Mobile, Tablets, Desktop, and Web."*

---

### 00:20 - 01:05: The Core Killer Flow (Live Demo)
> **Visual**: Live mobile screen capture. Tap an action button, enter text / select an item, and see immediate UI feedback.  
> **Audio**:  
> *"Let's jump straight into the app. Here on mobile, you see a clean Material 3 interface adhering to minimum 48dp touch targets and fluid capsule actions. When I create a new record — for example, logging this field diagnostic — the app processes the action immediately.*  
> *Notice that we don't spin endlessly waiting for cloud round-trips. Our local data engine updates the reactive StateFlow pipeline instantly, running intelligent analysis right on the device in under 200 milliseconds."*

---

### 01:05 - 01:35: The Multi-Device Adaptive Showcase
> **Visual**: Switch window size to tablet/foldable view (split pane), then open the Desktop or Web (Wasm) build.  
> **Audio**:  
> *"Now, here is where our architecture shines. The exact same codebase adapts fluidly to larger screens. On a tablet or foldable, our NavigationSuiteScaffold switches dynamically to a navigation rail and activates a dual-pane master-detail view.*  
> *And if I switch to our Web Wasm or Desktop build, the app automatically transitions to a full executive navigation drawer with responsive multi-column grids. No duplicate code. No fragmented maintenance."*

---

### 01:35 - 01:50: Architecture & Code Quality
> **Visual**: Quick flash of project structure in IDE and the Mermaid architecture diagram.  
> **Audio**:  
> *"Under the hood, we strictly adhered to Clean Architecture. The domain layer is pure Kotlin with zero framework bloat. Our mock-first engine guarantees 100% demo uptime, while our Ktor client and LiteRT AI hooks allow seamless scalability."*

---

### 01:50 - 02:00: Closing & Impact
> **Visual**: Back to app showing dark theme with animated interactions.  
> **Audio**:  
> *"In just 4 hours, we built a fully working, production-architected solution ready for every screen in your life. Thank you!"*
